## Services and content shown by the app

The app reads public data from Open-Meteo (weather), ExchangeRate-API (exchange rates, attribution shown in the widget), ESPN's public scoreboard, Unsplash and Wallhaven (pictures, credit shown on screen), Apple's iTunes feeds, Google's YouTube API and the news feeds of the publishers listed in the News widget.
